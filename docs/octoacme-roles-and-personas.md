# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers**: Report progress, blockers, and risks
- **With QA/Testing**: Coordinate testing, fix defects collaboratively
- **With DevOps Engineers**: Coordinate deployments and infrastructure needs
- **With UX/UI Designers**: Implement design specs and provide technical feasibility input

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Align on scope, timeline, and dependencies
- **With Developers**: Discuss technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With UX/UI Designers**: Validate user experience and feature design
- **With Business Analysts**: Detail requirements and acceptance criteria
- **With Customer Support Advocates**: Incorporate user feedback into roadmap

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Align on priorities and scope changes
- **With Developers**: Track progress, identify blockers, escalate risks
- **With Scrum Masters**: Partner on removing impediments and improving process
- **With DevOps Engineers**: Coordinate release and deployment windows
- **With Business Analysts**: Clarify requirements and scope boundaries

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and drive continuous improvement. They coach the team on agile practices and help optimize the delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team impediments and blockers
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor sprint progress and help the team stay focused
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives based on retrospective feedback
- Protect the team from external distractions and scope creep

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and effectiveness
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable self-organizing teams to achieve their commitments
- Reduce cycle time and delivery bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Facilitating sprint ceremonies (15–75 minutes each)
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Impediment logs and escalation reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate on timelines, escalate cross-team blockers
- **With Developers**: Support and unblock technical work
- **With Product Managers**: Help manage backlog refinement and sprint scope
- **With DevOps Engineers**: Facilitate deployment readiness and release coordination

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure that products are usable, accessible, and delightful. They conduct user research, create design specs, and collaborate with developers to deliver polished user experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validate design decisions
- Create wireframes, mockups, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and feasibility
- Conduct design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Document design systems and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maintain consistency across product surfaces
- Measure and improve user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with stakeholders
- Specifications and design documentation
- Collaboration with developers on technical feasibility
- User testing and feedback reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Validate feature concepts and user flows
- **With Developers**: Discuss technical implementation and design feasibility
- **With QA/Testing**: Define acceptance criteria for user experience
- **With Customer Support Advocates**: Incorporate user feedback into design iterations
- **With Business Analysts**: Clarify requirements and design constraints

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers own continuous integration, deployment automation, infrastructure, and release health. They enable fast, reliable delivery by building and maintaining the tools and processes that support the development pipeline.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, deployment environments, and scaling
- Ensure security scanning and compliance in the pipeline
- Monitor production systems and support incident response
- Optimize build times, test execution, and deployment speed
- Document runbooks and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable rapid, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment risk through automation
- Minimize time-to-recovery for incidents

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and release coordination
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation
- Incident response and post-mortem participation
- Performance and reliability dashboards

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Support infrastructure needs, debug pipeline failures
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment windows and release schedules
- **With QA/Testing**: Enable automated testing in the pipeline
- **With Scrum Masters**: Communicate deployment constraints and blockers

---

## Customer Support Advocate

### Role Summary
Customer Support Advocates represent end-user perspectives within the product team. They bridge the gap between customers and engineering by channeling user feedback, identifying pain points, and advocating for user-centered solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize customer feedback and support tickets
- Identify recurring user issues and pain points
- Advocate for user needs in product prioritization
- Participate in feature reviews and acceptance testing
- Document use cases and user scenarios
- Provide user perspective in design and technical discussions

### Goals
- Reduce support burden and user frustration
- Ensure customer voice is represented in product decisions
- Improve user satisfaction and product adoption
- Identify high-impact improvements based on real user needs

### Typical Communication
- Support ticket summaries and trend reports
- User feedback synthesis and recommendations
- Participation in feature acceptance and review
- User scenario documentation and examples

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Provide customer insights to inform roadmap prioritization
- **With Developers**: Explain user context and impact of issues
- **With UX/UI Designers**: Share user pain points and design feedback
- **With Business Analysts**: Clarify user requirements and use cases
- **With QA/Testing**: Define user-centered acceptance criteria

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. They detail requirements, validate assumptions, and ensure that delivered solutions align with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Create detailed specifications and acceptance criteria
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Identify and manage business risks and dependencies
- Support stakeholder communication and change management

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce rework and scope creep
- Improve clarity and shared understanding across teams
- Enable effective decision-making through data and analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification sessions
- Acceptance criteria definitions and validation reports
- Process flow diagrams and mapping documents

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Detail requirements and validate prioritization
- **With Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and resolve ambiguities
- **With Project Managers**: Assess impact of scope changes
- **With UX/UI Designers**: Ensure business requirements align with user experience
- **With Customer Support Advocates**: Validate business needs against real user scenarios
